# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 9 November 2026

NullSpacer is a minimal Android utility designed to create an invisible home-screen shortcut.

## Information Collection

NullSpacer does not intentionally collect, request, or transmit personal information.

The application does not implement:
- User accounts or registration.
- Advertising or analytics.
- Tracking or telemetry.
- Network-based application functionality.

## Permissions

The application does not declare any Android permissions in its manifest.

## Data Storage and Sharing

NullSpacer does not provide functionality for collecting or storing personal user data. It does not intentionally share personal information with third parties.

## Third-Party Services

The application does not integrate advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy will be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change.
